Our story

About Flair Academy

Flair Academy is an online learning institution founded on one belief: tutoring gets results. We support students from Grade 1 to Grade 12 in their own school curriculum, so what they learn with us goes straight into better understanding and better marks at school.

Every child learns differently. That is why every class at Flair Academy is one-on-one: the teacher works at your child’s pace, explains patiently and tracks progress closely, so no student is left behind and every student is encouraged to go further.

Our name says it all: flair is the natural talent every learner has. Our job is to help it shine.

“Every learner has a flair of their own. Our job is to help it shine.”
